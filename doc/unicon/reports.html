Technical Reports

These reports document aspects of Unicon, sometimes in more detail than the Unicon book, and sometimes with implementation developers in mind rather than end-users.

Canonical RST sources and built HTML for the reports live under doc/utr/ (HTML in doc/utr/html/). Older mirrors may still appear on unicon.org.
